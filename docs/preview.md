# Preview

> **Audience:** operator · **Profile:** `both` · **Read this when:** you are trying a feature the app lists under Preview: virtual desktops, directories or the Workload Lab.

Features the app marks as preview: they work, and their shape may still change. Each is
behind a flag in Settings → Preview features, and the app's **Preview** menu shows the
ones that are on.

| Page | Read it when |
|---|---|
| [Virtual Desktops](preview/virtual-desktops.md) | you need a pool of private desktop VMs that reps reach through the PRA Gateway instead of over the open internet |
| [Directories](preview/directories.md) | you want servers joined to Active Directory, to manage an on-premises AD or LDAP directory, or to manage groups in Entra ID, Okta or PingOne |
| [Workload Lab](preview/workload-lab.md) | something that is not a person needs a credential, and you want to pick the mechanism before reading any one guide |
