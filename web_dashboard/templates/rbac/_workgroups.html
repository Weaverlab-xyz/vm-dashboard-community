{# Workgroups -- the fourth tab of the RBAC page, and the only one that is not admin-only. The other three tabs answer "what may this principal DO?"; this one answers "to WHICH objects?". `docs/permissions.md` has always opened by calling those two questions the whole model, and then sent the reader to two different pages to answer them. **This tab is reachable by a non-admin and the other three are not.** 7 of the 8 /api/workgroups routes are gated on the grantable `workgroups` scope rather than require_admin (only DELETE needs the flag), and this page carried no admin redirect at all -- so somebody holding `workgroups:read` has always been able to use it. That is why rbacPage()'s gate is per-TAB rather than per-page: a blanket admin check here would have quietly revoked access that already existed. See main.py's /workgroups route. No permission_matrix import: this axis is objects, not scopes, and it must not grow one. Script inline in this file, like the other partials -- an included partial cannot own a Jinja block (named without spelling the tag, because a source sweep for one would otherwise match this sentence). #}

A workgroup is the other half of access control: the permission tabs decide what someone may do, and a workgroup decides which VMs, databases and clusters they may do it to. Each cloud resource is tagged with its workgroup, and a user sees only the ones they are assigned to. Administrators see everything. Feature guide.
