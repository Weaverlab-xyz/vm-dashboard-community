The only tab here that a non-admin can reach -- see rbacPage()'s per-tab gate. USERS AND GROUPS WERE TWO PAGES AND ARE NOT ANY MORE, because a person's effective access is the UNION of what both of them say. Answering "what can this user do?" meant holding two pages open and merging them in your head, and the two grids drifted apart twice before they were extracted into partials/permission_matrix.html. A group mapping is a user-provisioning rule; it belongs beside the users it provisions. THREE ROUTES REACH THIS TEMPLATE -- see main.py. /rbac is the nav entry; /users and /groups are still real routes that open their own tab, and they are NOT leftover aliases. Four operator runbooks and two integration guides print those paths as things to open, and settings.html links /groups from the single-sign-on panel. A 301 would also lose the query string, and a redirect chain on a path somebody pasted from a runbook is a worse answer than a page. NOTE what is deliberately ABSENT, because the /agents precedent this copies has it and a reader will look for it: there is NO per-tab feature gate, no single-tab retitle case and no tab-bar suppression. Nothing here is: /api/users, /api/groups and /api/roles are all always mounted and all require_admin.
