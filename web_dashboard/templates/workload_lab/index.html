Workload Lab

Preview

The labs for identities that belong to machines. There is no human in any of these workflows by design — the consumer is a pipeline, a broker or a cluster, not a person. A certificate, a SPIFFE identity, a Kubernetes token and a short-lived cloud credential are four answers to the same question, which is why they share a page — and which one an operator can actually use depends on what the consumer is and who runs the thing it is reaching.
